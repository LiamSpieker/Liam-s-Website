The Fool’s Errand · a blog series

taking the unserious seriously

The Fool’s Errand is an ongoing exploration of American college humor magazines, satire newspapers, and campus mischief.

This is an unfinished archive of the publications students made and the little worlds that formed around them.

A project by Liam Spieker, former editor-in-chief of the Dailyer Nebraskan.
